# Usage Insights Beta — initiative brief

## Outcome

Give enterprise administrators a weekly usage dashboard that helps them identify adoption gaps before renewal conversations.

## Q3 success metrics

- Five design partners receive beta access by September 15.
- 80% of beta administrators open the dashboard weekly.
- Data freshness is below 24 hours for 95% of accounts.
- No account-level data is exposed across tenants.

## Scope

The beta includes weekly active users, feature adoption by workspace, CSV export, and configurable aggregation. It excludes user-level behavioral drill-downs and external data warehouse export.

## Current risk

The analytics warehouse can produce the requested account-level aggregates, but raw-event retention is under Security and Legal review. Do not promise user-level reporting until that review closes.
