# Enterprise SSO GA

Goal: launch SAML SSO for ten design partners by September 30.

Success means at least eight partners activate, median setup takes less than fifteen minutes, authentication succeeds at least 97%, and there are no P0 authentication incidents.

Decision D-04: the v1 setup will be self-serve. Administrators will upload IdP metadata and complete setup without support. General availability remains gated on Security review.

Acme and Globex are the first pilot partners. Password login must remain available throughout rollout.
