# Northstar Cloud — Q3 company OKRs

## Objective O1: Earn enterprise trust in the core platform

- KR1: Activate ten design partners on Enterprise SSO by September 30.
- KR2: Complete identity threat-model review before general availability.
- KR3: Maintain 99.95% authentication availability and zero P0 identity incidents.

## Objective O2: Make product usage measurable and actionable

- KR1: Give five design partners access to Usage Insights beta by September 15.
- KR2: Reach 80% weekly active dashboard use among beta administrators.
- KR3: Publish data retention and aggregation controls before beta expansion.

## Objective O3: Make integrations predictable for strategic partners

- KR1: Migrate three partners from Partner API v1 to v2 by September 20.
- KR2: Keep API migration-related support tickets below five per week.
- KR3: Complete API v1 deprecation communication and rollback plan.

## Portfolio rule

No initiative may claim a company KR is at risk without naming the evidence, accountable function, and next decision or action.
